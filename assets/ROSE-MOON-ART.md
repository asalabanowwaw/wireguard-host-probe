# Rose & Moon artwork

Generated for Diana using the user's supplied light and dark panel references as visual guidance. These are illustration-only assets, without baked-in UI labels, controls or metrics. No reference screenshots are used as page backgrounds.

- `rose-moon-dark.webp`: midnight violet anime observatory, silver-haired woman, black cat, lantern and moonlit castle; empty space for live heading on the left.
- `rose-moon-light.webp`: parchment, burgundy and antique gold variation of the same composition.

Generated PNGs were converted to WebP for delivery; artwork composition is unchanged. The two themes and their assets are embedded into `panel.html` by `build_panel.py`.
