# Original dashboard artwork

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this project. No franchise character or third-party illustration is used. The original PNG is retained in the image-generation output directory; `anime-hero.webp` is a compressed format conversion for deployment. The deployable panel embeds this artwork so older updaters do not need additional asset paths.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide background illustration for an anime-inspired WireGuard admin dashboard. Create an original cinematic anime night scene, panoramic 3:1 composition: a lone adult female network guardian with long silver hair, wearing a modest dark technical jacket and headphones, occupies the right third, looking across a rainy futuristic Japanese city with delicate sakura petals and distant magenta and cyan city lights. Left two thirds are mostly deep midnight navy atmospheric negative space suitable for overlaying clear dashboard text, low detail and low contrast there. Elegant hand-painted anime film background with crisp restrained character linework, sophisticated lavender and dusty pink accents, soft reflected moonlight. Not chibi, not sexualized, not a franchise character. No typography, no logos, no interface elements, no watermarks. The character is environmental artwork, not the dominant UI. Opaque background.
